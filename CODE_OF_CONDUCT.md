# Community guidelines

Participate constructively, be respectful, and focus on reproducible technical feedback. Harassment, discriminatory behavior, personal attacks, spam and deliberate disclosure of another person's private data are not acceptable.

Do not upload real Home Assistant configurations, unredacted device logs or credentials when requesting support. A maintainer may remove sensitive posts to protect users.

Report security incidents privately through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Repository maintainers may moderate comments, issues and contributions to preserve a safe and useful project.
